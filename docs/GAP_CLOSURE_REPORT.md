# Gap Closure Report — v2.4.0-rc.3

The v2.4.0 upgrade closes the internal-practicality gaps that previously kept the 15 requested layers below 10/10.

## Major closures

- **Master Data:** dependency/impact controls and historical references.
- **Allocation:** arbitrary stage graph, cycle protection, composite drivers, recipient eligibility/benefit/cap/FX controls.
- **Benchmark:** comparable-level observations and deterministic quartiles.
- **P&L:** cube, snapshots and locking.
- **True-Up:** target selection, batch and governed reversal.
- **Accounting:** template-driven journal generation and balance controls.
- **Exceptions:** operational remediation/waiver lifecycle and automatic creation.
- **Evidence:** metadata/object linkage and audit-pack generation.
- **Audit:** transaction/correlation reconstruction.
- **Approvals:** reusable version-pinned engine with return/withdraw/resubmit and escalation.
- **Policies:** full editable governed lifecycle and impact/history.
- **Variance:** expanded comparisons and source-driven decomposition with explicit residual.
- **Reconciliation:** stage controls, tolerance, exception creation and sign-off.
- **Security:** scoped hashed service credentials plus backend enforcement.
- **Qualification:** evidence-backed current-release registry.

Remaining items are external qualification or broader engineering metrics, not missing internal business-layer workflows.
